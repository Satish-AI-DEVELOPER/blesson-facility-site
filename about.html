About Us

Built to Be the One Maintenance Vendor You Don't Have to Manage

Blesson Facility exists because corporate facilities teams shouldn't have to coordinate five different contractors for carpentry, electrical, plumbing and painting.

Our Story

Facility Maintenance, Built Around Corporate & Banking Standards

Blesson Facility was established to bring a single, accountable standard to commercial facility maintenance — the kind that banks, NBFCs and corporate offices in India actually need: background-verified staff, documented visits, and one point of contact across every trade.

Rather than treating carpentry, electrical, plumbing, gypsum work, painting and civil repairs as separate vendor relationships, we run them as one coordinated service — so your facilities team gets predictable response times and consistent quality, whether it's a single branch or a multi-city retail chain.
