General Maintenance for Corporate & Bank Facilities

Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts for offices, banks and retail units

Get a Free Quote WhatsApp Us

Corporate offices, bank branches and retail outlets run on dozens of small systems that quietly need attention every week — air conditioning, false ceilings, flooring, door and window hardware, signage, and general wear and tear. Left unmanaged, these small issues become downtime, safety risks, or awkward moments in front of your own customers.

Blesson Facility's General Maintenance AMC brings all of this under a single, predictable contract. Our technicians run scheduled preventive rounds, log every visit, and respond to breakdown calls with an agreed turnaround time — so your internal team gets one point of contact instead of juggling five different vendors.

What's Included

General Maintenance Coverage

Preventive inspection rounds

Scheduled walk-throughs that catch wear, leaks and faults before they become breakdowns or safety issues.

Single point of accountability

One AMC, one service desk, one escalation path — instead of managing separate vendors for every trade.

HVAC & fixture upkeep

Routine servicing of air-conditioning units, false ceilings, doors, windows, signage and interior fittings.

Breakdown calls are logged and attended within agreed SLAs, with escalation for critical/bank-priority sites.

Verified technician teams

Digital visit reporting

Photo-based visit reports and checklists so your facilities manager has a clear audit trail.

FAQs

General Maintenance — Common Questions

What does a General Maintenance AMC with Blesson Facility include?

It covers preventive inspection rounds, HVAC and fixture upkeep, hardware and fitting repairs, and breakdown response — scoped to your specific premises during onboarding. Electrical, plumbing, carpentry and civil work can be bundled into the same AMC or handled as separate contracts, whichever suits your facilities budget.

Can you service multiple branches or offices under one contract?

Yes. Many of our corporate and banking clients run a single master AMC across multiple locations in Delhi NCR and Central India, with consolidated reporting and one point of contact for all sites.

Do your technicians work in high-security environments like bank branches?

Yes. Our teams are background-verified and briefed on the ID, escort and access protocols that bank branches, ATMs and secure corporate floors typically require.
