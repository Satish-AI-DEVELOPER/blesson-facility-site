Plumbing

Plumbing for Corporate & Bank Facilities

Leak repairs, sanitation maintenance and water management for commercial premises

Get a Free Quote WhatsApp Us

Washroom and plumbing complaints are among the fastest-escalating facility issues — they affect hygiene, staff morale and how visiting clients perceive your office or branch. Yet they're often handled reactively, by whoever's available.

Blesson Facility runs plumbing and sanitation maintenance as a structured service: fixture checks, leak repairs, drainage clearing and water tank hygiene, so washrooms and utility areas stay presentable and functional without last-minute scrambling.

What's Included

Plumbing Coverage

Leak detection & repair

Fast response to visible and hidden leaks in taps, pipelines, and washroom fittings.

Washroom sanitation maintenance

Fixture servicing, flush systems and drainage upkeep for staff and client-facing washrooms.

Water tank & supply checks

Periodic inspection of storage tanks and supply lines for hygiene and consistent pressure.

Drainage & blockage clearing

Clearing slow or blocked drains before they become a bigger sanitation issue.

Fixture replacement

Repair or replacement of taps, flush valves, sanitaryware and washroom hardware.

Scheduled preventive checks

Regular plumbing rounds included in AMC to catch small leaks before they cause damage.

How It Works

Our Process

1. Report or scheduled inspection

Log an issue, or have plumbing included in your regular AMC inspection rounds.

2. On-site diagnosis

Our plumbers trace the source of leaks or blockages rather than treating only the visible symptom.

3. Repair or fixture replacement

Using quality fittings suited to commercial-grade daily use.

4. Post-job check

A follow-up check to confirm the fix holds under normal usage.

FAQs

Plumbing — Common Questions

Can plumbing maintenance be bundled with general AMC?

Yes, plumbing and sanitation checks are commonly included as part of a General Maintenance AMC, with dedicated visits for washroom-heavy sites like retail stores and branches.

Do you handle water tank cleaning and hygiene checks?

Yes, periodic water tank inspection and cleaning coordination can be scheduled as part of your maintenance plan.

How fast can you respond to a leak at a bank branch?

Leaks are treated as priority calls, especially at sites with server rooms, ATMs or currency areas nearby — response times are agreed as part of your AMC SLA.
