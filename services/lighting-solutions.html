Lighting Solutions

LED Lighting Supply, Installation & Maintenance

Downlights, spotlights, grille lights and track lights sourced, fitted and serviced for offices, banks and retail stores — as a standalone job or bundled into your AMC.

Get a Free Quote WhatsApp Us

Poor or inconsistent lighting is one of the fastest ways an office, branch or store looks tired to a visitor — and one of the easiest to fix. Our lighting teams supply and fit LED downlights, spotlights and track lights, and handle the ongoing maintenance so fittings keep working the way they did on day one.

LED Fittings We Supply & Install

Round Adjustable COB LED Downlight

Square COB LED Downlight
