Electrical

Electrical for Corporate & Bank Facilities

Certified electrical maintenance, panel work and lighting for commercial buildings

Get a Free Quote WhatsApp Us

Electrical issues in a commercial building carry real risk — to safety, to equipment, and to business continuity for a bank branch or a retail counter that can't afford downtime. Ad-hoc electricians without facility experience often patch the symptom, not the cause.

Blesson Facility's electrical teams are trained on commercial-building work: distribution panels, UPS and backup points, lighting circuits, and the general wiring health of a live office. We prioritise safe isolation, proper documentation, and fixes that hold.

What's Included

Electrical Coverage

Panel & DB maintenance

Routine checks and servicing of distribution boards and panels to catch loose connections and overloads early.

Wiring repair & fault-finding

Diagnosing and fixing intermittent faults, tripping circuits and dead points without guesswork.

Lighting maintenance & upgrades

Replacement, repair and energy-efficient LED upgrades for office, branch and retail lighting.

UPS / backup point support

Coordination around UPS and backup power points to keep critical systems like servers and ATMs running.

Safety-first isolation

Proper lock-out procedures and safe working practices for live commercial environments.

Emergency breakdown response

Priority attendance for electrical faults that affect safety or business operations.

How It Works

Our Process

1. Fault report or audit

Log a breakdown call, or request a proactive electrical health check of your premises.

2. Safe diagnosis

Our electricians isolate and diagnose the issue using proper safety procedure, not shortcuts.

3. Repair with documentation

Work is completed and logged, including any parts replaced, for your maintenance records.

4. Preventive follow-up

Recurring issues are flagged for inclusion in your AMC preventive schedule.

FAQs

Electrical — Common Questions

Can you respond to an urgent electrical fault outside office hours?

Yes — emergency and after-hours response is available for AMC clients, and can be arranged for one-time call-outs depending on location and urgency.

Do you handle panel and DB-level work, or only surface wiring?

Both. Our electricians handle distribution panel checks and minor panel work in addition to general wiring, lighting and fixture-level repairs.

Can you help reduce our lighting energy costs?

Yes, we assess existing lighting and recommend LED or energy-efficient upgrades where the payback makes sense for your site.
