Privacy Policy

How Blesson Facility collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

This policy explains how Blesson Facility ("we", "us") handles information submitted through this website's inquiry form, WhatsApp chat, phone or email.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, company, email, phone number, city and a description of your requirement — solely to respond to your request and provide a quote or service.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share to respond to inquiries, schedule site visits, prepare quotations, and deliver maintenance services. We do not sell your information to third parties.
