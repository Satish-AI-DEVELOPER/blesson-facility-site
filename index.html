About Blesson Facility

A Single, Accountable Maintenance Partner for Commercial Infrastructure

Blesson Facility is a dedicated maintenance partner for corporate offices, banks, NBFCs and retail chains, providing carpentry, electrical, plumbing, gypsum & partition, painting, civil and general maintenance work.

We understand the access and security protocols banks and secure corporate floors require, and the tight turnaround retail stores need to stay presentable. Our background-verified teams work to agreed SLAs, so your facility keeps running without you having to manage multiple vendors.

FAQs

Common Questions

What areas does Blesson Facility serve?

We're based in the Delhi NCR region and actively serve corporate, banking and retail clients across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram, Faridabad, Lucknow, Agra, Meerut, Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore and Jabalpur.

What types of clients does Blesson Facility work with?

Primarily banks and NBFC branches, corporate and MNC offices, IT parks, retail stores and showrooms, and healthcare facilities — anywhere that needs dependable, background-verified maintenance support.

Can all your services be combined into a single AMC?

Yes. General maintenance, carpentry, electrical, plumbing, gypsum & partition, painting and civil work can all be scoped under one Annual Maintenance Contract, so you deal with a single vendor instead of five.

How fast can you respond to an urgent maintenance issue?

AMC clients get priority response with agreed SLAs, including 24/7 support for critical sites like bank branches. For one-time requests, response time depends on location and urgency — contact us and we'll confirm directly.

Is Blesson Facility GST and MSME registered?

Yes, Blesson Facility is GST registered and MSME/Udyam registered, and our field staff are background-verified before deployment.

Ready to fix a maintenance headache — or prevent the next one?

Talk to Blesson Facility about an AMC or a one-time job. Replies within one business day, faster for urgent requests.
